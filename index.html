How to play

  1. Aim and drop. Click or tap a prize, or line the claw up and press Drop claw.
  2. Keep pressure in the marked band. Hold to squeeze, release to loosen. Every prize likes a different grip.
  3. Bring home three prizes. Bonuses for a dead-centre drop (Bullseye), a grip that never leaves the band (Perfect), and captures in a row (Streak).

Keyboard: focus the chamber or the main button, then use ← → to aim, Space to drop, and hold Space to grip.

Grip pressure is shown on a visual meter, so the game needs sight to play well. Results and turn changes are announced to screen readers.
